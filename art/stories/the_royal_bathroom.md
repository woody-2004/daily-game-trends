# The Royal Bathroom

*A petty-revenge fruit comedy. Based on a story about a family vacation, retold and dramatized.*

**Runtime:** about 2 × 75 seconds, or one cut of about 2½ minutes.
**Style:** Hyper-realistic 3D Pixar-style fruit people, cozy lake-house lighting, reality-TV confessional cutaways.

## Cast

| Character | Fruit | Vibe |
|---|---|---|
| **Kiki** (narrator) | Kiwi | Single, chill, sees everything, says nothing |
| **Pearl** (sister) | Peach | "Princess" energy, always arrives first |
| **Mom & Dad** | Two old melons | Just happy everyone's together |
| **The kids** | Blueberries (ages 1–25) | Chaos, everywhere, all the time |

*Running gag:* Kiki talks to the camera in "confessional" cutaways, like a reality show, sitting on a basement stairway with one flickering bulb.

---

## PART 1: "The Room"

**[0:00] HOOK: slow push-in on a toilet paper roll, dramatic music**

> **NARRATOR (Kiki):** Five days. Fourteen family members. One vacation house.
> And the sweetest revenge of my life... cost me **absolutely nothing.**

*On-screen text:* **"My sister stole the best room… so I let the house get revenge 🧻"**

**[0:07] The tradition**

*Aerial shot: a giant lake house. Cars pull in from every direction. A swarm of blueberry kids pours out.*

> **NARRATOR:** Every year my whole family rents a huge house together. We live ten hours apart, so this is the one week we're all under one roof. Parents, siblings, spouses, nieces, nephews... from age 1 to age 25.

**[0:18] The rules**

> **NARRATOR:** The room rules are simple. Couples and families with little kids pick first. Singles like me pick last. Fair enough.

**[0:25] The arrival**

*Pearl the peach bursts through the front door in sunglasses and a sun hat, rolling FOUR suitcases.*

> **NARRATOR:** My sister Pearl got there first. She always gets there first.
> Her husband was supposed to come... but his new job wouldn't let him off.

*Pearl opens the best bedroom: a king bed, a view of the lake, and its own private bathroom. Angels sing.*

> **NARRATOR:** She claimed the best room anyway. Right next to the lounge. Big bed. Private bathroom.

**[0:40] The confrontation (sort of)**

*Kiki stands in the doorway with a backpack. Pearl is already unpacked, sipping a smoothie.*

> **PEARL:** I'm totally open to hearing why *you* should have this room.
> *(pause)*
> **PEARL:** ...Go ahead.

*Crickets. Kiki opens his mouth. Closes it. Opens it again.*

> **KIKI (confessional):** She had me. I had nothing. Zero arguments. She's family, she got there first. Fair's fair.

**[0:55] The basement**

*Kiki walks down creaky basement stairs. One flickering bulb. A tiny window. A bed. No bathroom.*

> **NARRATOR:** My room was in the basement. It was fine! Honestly. Just... no bathroom.

*A spider waves at him from the corner. He waves back.*

**[1:05] Cliffhanger**

> **NARRATOR:** So I let it go. Completely.
> Until day two... when I discovered **the secret of the house.**

*Freeze-frame: Kiki's eyes going wide in a hallway bathroom.*

*On-screen text:* **"Part 2 → the secret 🧻👀"**

## PART 2: "The Secret"

**[0:00] HOOK**

> **NARRATOR:** Some revenge you plan. Some revenge... just *falls into your lap.*

**[0:05] The discovery**

*Kiki walks into the hallway bathroom. A golden glow. He picks up the toilet paper roll. It's thick, fluffy, quilted. A choir sings.*

> **NARRATOR:** On day one I noticed the house had the **good** toilet paper. The soft, fluffy, expensive kind. Classy.

**[0:15] The investigation**

*Montage: Kiki casually checking every bathroom. Upstairs: fluffy. Hallway: fluffy. Kids' bathroom: fluffy.*

> **NARRATOR:** Then on day two, I noticed something. Every bathroom in the house had the good stuff.

*Kiki peeks through a cracked door into Pearl's private bathroom. Close-up on her roll: thin, see-through, single-ply. It crackles like sandpaper when he touches it.*

> **NARRATOR:** Every bathroom... **except one.**

*Dramatic zoom. Thunder. The spider in the basement gasps.*

**[0:30] The decision**

> **KIKI (confessional, slowly smiling):** Now... a good brother would have said something.

*Long pause.*

> **KIKI (confessional):** Anyway.

**[0:40] The vacation montage**

*Five days of fun: lake swims, board games, a blueberry toddler eating sand, Dad burning burgers. And every time...*

> **NARRATOR:** And because her bathroom was *so* convenient... she never once used another one. Not once. Five days.

*Pearl strolls into her private bathroom, head held high.*
*Cut to Kiki at the dinner table, sipping lemonade, eyes following her. He doesn't blink.*
*Pearl comes out, walking just a little... stiffly.*

> **PEARL:** Is it just me, or is this house kind of... rough?
> **KIKI:** *(innocently)* Rough? I think it's lovely.

**[1:00] The last day**

*Everyone hugs goodbye in the driveway.*

> **PEARL:** Best vacation ever! Same house next year?
> **KIKI:** Absolutely. And you should *definitely* take the same room.

*Pearl smiles, having no idea.*

**[1:10] The ending**

> **NARRATOR:** She had the big bed. She had the view. She had the private bathroom.
> And for five days... she had the worst toilet paper in the entire state.

*Final confessional: Kiki holding a roll of the fluffy stuff like a trophy.*

> **KIKI:** So yes, Pearl. Enjoy your private bathroom.

*Beat.*

> **KIKI:** And your *crappy* toilet paper. 🧻

*On-screen text:* **"Would YOU have told her? 👇"**
