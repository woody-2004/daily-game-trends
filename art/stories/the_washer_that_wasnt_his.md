# The Washer That Wasn't His

*A fruit-drama retelling in 3 parts, inspired by a viral r/MaliciousCompliance post ("Landlord refused to fix appliances, so I sold them"). Retold and dramatized, not copied.*

**Runtime:** about 3 × 75–90 seconds (TikTok, Reels or Shorts series), or one cut of about 4 minutes.
**Style:** Hyper-realistic 3D Pixar-style fruit people, telenovela lighting, dramatic zooms.

## Cast

| Character | Fruit | Vibe |
|---|---|---|
| **Rosie** (narrator) | Strawberry | Sweet, hard-working, too polite... until she isn't |
| **Mr. Pineton** (landlord) | Pineapple | Fat, smug, gold chain, spiky crown, always eating |
| **Mr. & Mrs. Vine** (old tenants) | Grape couple | Tired, bitter, full of warnings |
| **Old Mrs. Coco** (neighbor) | Coconut | Knows *everything* on the street |
| **Benny** (buyer) | Banana | Cheerful guy with a pickup truck |

---

## PART 1: "The Fire"

**[0:00] HOOK: black screen, then a close-up of a dryer bursting into flames**

> **NARRATOR (Rosie):** My landlord told me the washer and dryer weren't his.
> So on the day I moved out... I sold them.
> And what he did next? Oh, you're gonna want to hear this.

*On-screen text:* **"My landlord said it wasn't his… so I sold it 😈"**

**[0:08] Flashback: sunny street, a perfect house**

> **NARRATOR:** One year earlier. We'd just found the *perfect* house. Big windows. Quiet street. And a laundry room with the fanciest washer and gas dryer I had ever seen. The landlord, Mr. Pineton, handed me the keys with this huge smile...

*Mr. Pineton, a pineapple in a shiny suit, jingles the keys, gold teeth glinting.*

> **MR. PINETON:** Treat her nice, sweetheart. She's a special house.

> **NARRATOR:** I should've known. Nobody smiles like that unless they're about to rob you.

**[0:25] The fire**

*A month later. Rosie hums, loading her favourite clothes: her mom's sweater, her work uniform, a dress with the tags still on.*

> **NARRATOR:** One month in, I put in a load of laundry. My favourite dress. My work clothes. The sweater my mom knitted me before she passed.

*Smoke. A burning smell. Rosie opens the dryer, and black smoke pours out. The clothes are charred, still glowing at the edges.*

> **NARRATOR:** Everything. Burnt. Not *shrunk*. Not *faded*. **Burnt.** Like the dryer was trying to cremate them.

*Rosie holds up the melted sweater, a tear rolling down her strawberry cheek.*

**[0:45] The email**

> **NARRATOR:** I took photos of everything and emailed Mr. Pineton. I was polite. I just asked him to fix the dryer and help cover the clothes. About three hundred dollars.

*Cut to Mr. Pineton lounging by a pool on a flamingo float, reading his phone, laughing.*

> **MR. PINETON (reading his reply out loud):** "The lease is very clear. It does NOT include a washer or dryer. If those appliances are in the house, they were left by a previous tenant. They are **not mine**. I am not responsible for anything I did not provide."

*He sips a drink with a tiny umbrella in it.*

> **NARRATOR:** Not his. The washer and dryer that were in the house when he showed it to me. That he *bragged about* on the tour. **Not. His.**

**[1:05] Cliffhanger**

> **NARRATOR:** So I paid for the repair myself. I replaced my clothes myself. And I swallowed it.
> But three months later... I met the family who lived there before me.
> And what they told me changed *everything*.

*Freeze-frame on the grape couple at a coffee shop, looking at each other, horrified.*

*On-screen text:* **"Part 2 → the old tenants' secret 👀"**

## PART 2: "The Warning"

**[0:00] HOOK**

> **NARRATOR:** If your landlord ever says "that's not mine"... *remember those words.* Because I did.

**[0:05] Coffee shop**

*Rosie sits across from Mr. and Mrs. Vine, two tired grapes.*

> **MRS. VINE:** You're renting from *Pineton*? Oh, honey.
> **MR. VINE:** We lived there four years. We painted every room. Fixed the fence. Redid the whole garden with our own money.
> **MRS. VINE:** We left that house nicer than we found it.
> **ROSIE:** So you got your deposit back, right?

*Long pause. The grapes laugh, bitter and exhausted.*

> **MR. VINE:** Not. One. Cent.
> **MRS. VINE:** He said the paint was the "wrong shade of white."

> **NARRATOR:** The *wrong shade of white.*

**[0:30] The neighbor**

*Old Mrs. Coco, a coconut in curlers and a bathrobe, leans over the fence with a watering can.*

> **MRS. COCO:** Sweetie, that man has **never** given back a deposit. Not once. Not in twenty years. I've watched every family on this street leave crying.
> **MRS. COCO:** You want my advice? *(leans in and whispers)* **Read. The. Lease.** Every. Single. Word. That's how he gets you... and that's how you get *him.*

*Dramatic zoom on Rosie's face. Thunder outside (on a sunny day, for comedy).*

**[0:50] The investigation montage**

*Late night. Rosie at the kitchen table: coffee, a highlighter, the lease under a desk lamp. Pages fly. Coffee cups pile up.*

> **NARRATOR:** So I read the lease. Once. Twice. Ten times. And I found two sentences.

*Close-up, highlighted in yellow:*
> **Line 1:** *"No appliances are included in this rental."*
> **Line 2:** *"Tenant is responsible for all reasonable costs to remove, haul, store or dispose of any property left behind."*

> **NARRATOR:** He said they weren't his. The lease said they weren't his. And the lease said anything left behind was *my* problem to get rid of.
> So I made a decision.

*Rosie closes the lease slowly and smiles for the first time. It is not a nice smile.*

**[1:10] Cliffhanger**

> **NARRATOR:** I didn't say a word. I paid my rent on time, every month. I smiled at him. I waved at him.
> And I waited... ten... more... months.

*On-screen text:* **"Part 3 → Move-out day 🔥"**

## PART 3: "Move-Out Day"

**[0:00] HOOK**

> **NARRATOR:** Two hours. That's how long it took for my landlord to realize what I'd done.

**[0:05] The sale**

*Rosie takes a photo of the washer and dryer and posts a listing: "Premium washer + gas dryer, like new, must go! Pick up Saturday."*

> **NARRATOR:** A week before move-out, I listed the washer and dryer online. Priced to sell.

*Benny the banana pulls up in a rusty pickup truck, grinning.*

> **BENNY:** You're selling these for *this* price? Are they stolen?
> **ROSIE:** Nope. According to my landlord, they belong to nobody.
> **BENNY:** ...I love this.

*Slow-motion shot: the washer and dryer loaded onto the truck like a heist movie, sunglasses on, epic music.*

**[0:25] Handing over the keys**

*Rosie hands the keys to Mr. Pineton, sweet as pie.*

> **ROSIE:** Thanks for everything, Mr. Pineton!
> **MR. PINETON:** *(already scribbling on his clipboard)* We'll see about that deposit, sweetheart. I noticed a scuff on the doorframe...

> **NARRATOR:** Of course he did.

**[0:35] Two hours later**

*Mr. Pineton opens the laundry room door. Empty. Just two clean rectangles on the floor where the machines used to be. A tumbleweed rolls by.*

*His eyes bulge. His crown shakes. His face turns bright red.*

> **MR. PINETON:** **WHERE. IS. MY. WASHER.**

*Rosie's phone buzzes: 1 new voicemail. She plays it on speaker. It's pure screaming.*

> **MR. PINETON (voicemail):** You bring back MY washer and MY dryer RIGHT NOW or I'm calling the police! Those are MY appliances! Do you know what those COST?!

*Rosie calmly sips her tea.*

**[0:55] The reply**

> **NARRATOR:** I didn't call him back. I just replied to his old email. The one from the day my clothes burned.

*On screen, the email being typed:*

> *"Hi Mr. Pineton, I got your voicemail, but I'm not sure which appliances you mean.*
> *As you told me in writing, the lease does not include a washer or dryer, and any appliances in the house were 'not yours.'*
> *The lease also says tenants are responsible for removing any property left behind. So I removed it.*
> *You're welcome. 🍓"*

*Mr. Pineton reads it. His own words, quoted back at him. Zoom on his face. Zoom. ZOOM.*

*His leaves droop one by one.*

**[1:15] The ending**

> **NARRATOR:** Did he give me my deposit back? Of course not. He found a "scuff," a "stain," and a "suspicious smell."
> But here's the thing...
> What I sold those machines for covered the deposit, the dryer repair, every burnt piece of clothing... and a little extra.

*Rosie, Mrs. Coco and the Vines toast with juice boxes on the porch.*

> **MRS. COCO:** Twenty years I waited to see someone do that.

> **NARRATOR:** So if your landlord ever tells you *"that's not mine"*...
> Believe him.

*Final shot: Mr. Pineton alone in the empty laundry room, one clean sock drifting down from the ceiling.*

*On-screen text:* **"Would you have done the same? 👇"**
